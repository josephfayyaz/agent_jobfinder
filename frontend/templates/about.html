About Job Agent

Job Agent is a local-first career workspace that helps you turn real experience into targeted CVs, job-fit analysis, compliance material, and reusable application assets. It is designed to help users work from evidence, not from vague prompting, while keeping local workflows first-class.

What the software is for

A structured workspace for onboarding, job analysis, CV drafting, review, and approval.

Job Agent is built to help a candidate move from setup to evidence-backed application materials without losing control of the source facts. The app connects one provider, stores your working profile locally, analyzes manual job descriptions, proposes CV changes, supports review decisions, and prepares output that can be approved before download.

The long-term goal is not only faster drafting. It is a safer and more defensible workflow for CV creation, rewriting, fit scoring, and supporting artifacts such as compliance matrices and reviewable fact memory.

Privacy and provider model

Local runtimes remain first-class, while cloud providers are optional.

Local-first by default

Workspace data is stored on your machine by default. If you choose a local provider such as an OpenAI-compatible local gateway or Ollama, the main drafting path can stay fully local.

One provider at a time

The app uses one active provider for intake, scoring, and draft generation so behavior remains predictable and easier to audit when you review a CV or a fit score later.

Evidence before drafting

Setup exists to prevent invented facts and weak CV output. The app tries to collect the provider, identity, fact source, and search focus before it unlocks the main drafting workflow.
