What Job Agent does

Build a truthful application workspace before you draft anything

Job Agent is a local-first career workspace that turns your real experience into defensible CVs, targeted applications, and reviewable draft materials. It collects the provider, profile, fact sheet, and search focus first so the later manual job-description workflow starts from verified evidence rather than guesswork.

Private by default

All workspace data is saved on your local machine by default. If you choose a local provider, your profile and CV workflow can stay entirely inside your own environment.

What happens next

  • Connect one provider that will handle intake and drafting.
  • Save the candidate identity used across CVs and profile memory.
  • Upload a CV PDF or notes so the app can build a grounded fact sheet.
  • Review extracted facts before the job and CV workflow unlocks.

Why this setup exists

The goal is not to move slowly. The goal is to prevent weak extraction, invented facts, and low-trust CVs before you start the manual JD-to-draft flow.
