What Job Agent does

Job Agent is a local-first career workspace that helps you turn real experience into targeted CVs, job-fit analysis, and reusable application materials. It guides you through provider setup, profile capture, and evidence collection so the app can write from facts instead of guesses.

2 of 3

Private by default

Your workspace data stays on your machine by default. If you choose a local provider, your profile, CV workflow, and review loop can remain inside your own environment instead of a cloud account.

What you will set up now

In the next few minutes you will choose one AI provider, save your identity, upload your CV or notes, verify extracted facts, and set the search focus that drives later job-description scoring and CV creation.
