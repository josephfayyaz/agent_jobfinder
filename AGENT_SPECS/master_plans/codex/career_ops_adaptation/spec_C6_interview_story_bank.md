# Spec C6 — Interview Story Bank

## Goal

Turn approved evidence into reusable interview stories and outreach snippets.

## Inputs

- fact sheet
- verified candidate facts
- approved drafts
- accepted bullet clarifications

## Build

1. Story object
   - title
   - context
   - action
   - result
   - tools/skills
   - role family tags
   - confidence source

2. Extraction
   - extract candidate stories from approved drafts and verified facts
   - deduplicate near-identical stories

3. Reuse
   - interview prep page
   - cover letter evidence
   - thesis / outreach messaging

4. Trust model
   - mark whether a story is:
     - fact-sheet backed
     - verified-memory backed
     - draft-derived but unverified

## UX

Users should be able to answer:
- “Give me my best Python/ownership/stakeholder story”
- “Show one story for each role family I target”

## Success Metric

- faster interview prep
- stronger consistency between drafts and interview answers
